Methodology & provenance

Data: CollegeFootballData.com (all 134–136 FBS teams, 2019–2026: games, multi-book opening+closing lines, 198k drive records, play-by-play, talent, returning production, coaches, QB starts). Every pull is schema-checked and logged against an API budget ledger.

Discipline: every evaluation pre-registered before running; leakage ("poison the future") tests at the ingest, ratings, and simulation layers; two findings retracted this cycle when their own controls failed them (a CLV look-ahead artifact and a reverse-causation QB effect) — both documented in the repo, not deleted.

The headline honesty: pooled walk-forward margin MAE — model 13.2, closing line 12.2. The market is the better forecaster and every blend attempt failed its gate (recorded twice, including on a virgin holdout). This site exists to show what the model can support, with the case against always attached.
